THE DETAILS

Good to know.

Everything you need to prepare for your session.

How do I book?

Use the session request form. Your date is reserved after availability and booking details are confirmed.

Where are you based?

Midland, Odessa and surrounding West Texas communities. Travel is available by quote.

When will I receive my photographs?

Estimated delivery is 7–14 business days for standard sessions, 2–3 weeks for extended or event sessions, and 2–4 weeks for video. Your confirmed delivery date takes priority.

Can you help with posing?

Yes. I provide direction for posing, expression and movement.

What should I wear?

Choose outfits that suit your concept and feel comfortable. We can discuss wardrobe before the session.

Are RAW files included?

Packages specify edited deliverables. Discuss RAW files or additional edits before booking.

Do you offer studio sessions?

Studio arrangements are discussed when booking. Rental, permits and special sets may cost extra.

Can I reschedule?

Contact me as soon as possible. Terms and applicable fees are confirmed in your booking agreement.

Can I request a custom package?

Yes. Send your project details for a tailored quote.

What if you miss my call?

Send a text or leave a voicemail with your name and inquiry. I will return your call when available.

Delivery & turnaround.

Estimated delivery: 7–14 business days for standard portraits and graduation, 2–3 weeks for extended or event sessions, and 2–4 weeks for video. These are proposed estimates, subject to confirmation before booking. Complex editing and holidays may affect delivery. Tell me about any deadline when requesting your session.

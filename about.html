BEHIND THE LENS

Hi, I’m Ronald.

Photographer, videographer and creative storyteller behind The Prime Grid.

Every frame has a feeling.

I approach photography as a way to create atmosphere, express personality and preserve moments that matter. My work moves between cinematic portraits, fashion editorials, sports imagery and personal milestones.

I enjoy building a photograph from the ground up: concept, wardrobe, composition, light and color. Whether a quiet portrait or bold editorial, the aim is to create work that feels intentional and true to the person in front of the camera.

Based in West Texas, I work with individuals, families, athletes and creative teams wherever their stories take us. I welcome projects across the United States and internationally, with travel arranged in advance.

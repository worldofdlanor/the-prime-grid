02 / BEHIND THE LENS

A little about
the eye behind it.

I'm the photographer behind The Prime Grid, a Texas-based photography brand built around a simple idea: a good image should do more than look good. It should make you stop, notice, and feel something.

My work moves between people, machines, movement, landscapes, and the everyday scenes most of us walk past. I love the contrast between a carefully directed portrait and an unplanned moment that happens once and never quite the same way again.

I approach every session with curiosity and intention. Whether I'm photographing a family, a car, an athlete, or a brand, the goal is the same: images that feel personal, considered, and unmistakably yours.

I'm building The Prime Grid one story at a time. The equipment I use, my background, client stories, and a photograph of me will be added here as the portfolio grows.
